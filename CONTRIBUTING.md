# Enterprise AI Readiness Framework

## Purpose

The Enterprise AI Readiness Framework helps organizations evaluate whether an AI solution is ready for production by assessing technical, operational, governance, and business capabilities.

---

# Pillars

## 1. Business Alignment

**Purpose**

Ensures the AI solution solves a meaningful business problem with measurable outcomes.

---

## 2. Architecture & Design

**Purpose**

Ensures the AI system is designed to scale, integrate, and operate reliably in enterprise environments.

---

## 3. Knowledge & Intelligence

**Purpose**

Ensures the AI system can access the right information, reason effectively, and use tools appropriately.

---

## 4. Trust & Governance

**Purpose**

Ensures the AI system is secure, compliant, safe, and aligned with organizational policies.

---

## 5. Operations & Continuous Improvement

**Purpose**

Ensures the AI system can be monitored, evaluated, maintained, and continuously improved after deployment.
