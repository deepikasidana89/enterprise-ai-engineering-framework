# Vision

## Why does this framework exist?
There is no shortage of AI knowledge. There are countless blogs, reference architectures, vendor guides, and research papers. The challenge is that this knowledge is fragmented. As an engineering leader, I still don't have a practical playbook that tells me what questions to ask, what decisions to make, how to evaluate trade-offs, and whether my AI system is truly ready for production. I want to create that playbook.
