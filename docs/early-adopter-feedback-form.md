# EARF Pilot Feedback Form

Use these questions after a participant completes an EARF assessment.

## Assessment details

1. Participant ID
2. What type of AI system did you assess?
3. Which language, framework, or model provider did it use?
4. Which EARF version did you use?
5. How long did installation take?
6. How long did the assessment take?
7. Did you use a public, private, academic, or personal repository?

## Ratings

Use a 1–5 scale, where 1 means strongly disagree and 5 means strongly agree.

- EARF was easy to install.
- The documentation was easy to follow.
- The report was easy to understand.
- The evidence locations were useful.
- The recommendations were actionable.
- EARF helped me identify risks or gaps.
- I would use EARF again.
- I would recommend EARF to another engineer.

## Accuracy and impact

1. How many findings did you review?
2. How many findings were accurate?
3. Which findings were inaccurate or incomplete?
4. What important evidence did EARF miss?
5. Did EARF identify anything your existing process or checklist missed?
6. Did you make any engineering changes after reviewing the report?
7. If yes, which controls changed?
8. What was the readiness score before the changes?
9. What was the readiness score after the changes?
10. Did EARF change a design, security, governance, or production decision?
11. What was the most useful part of EARF?
12. What should be improved first?

## Testimonial

> In 2–4 sentences, what problem did EARF help you identify or think about, and how useful was it for your AI engineering work?

## Publication consent

- I agree that my feedback may be used anonymously.
- I agree that my name and role may be published.
- I agree that my organization may be named.
- I agree that the project may be described in general terms.
- I do not want my feedback published.

May EARF lightly edit your response for grammar while preserving its meaning?
