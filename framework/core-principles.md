# Core Principles

## 1. Production-Ready Mindset
AI systems must be production-ready from inception, not retrofitted for production. This means:
- Built with operational resilience in mind
- Designed for monitoring, logging, and observability
- Architected for scalability and reliability
- Compliance and governance baked in from day one

## 2. Holistic Assessment
Readiness is not just about ML model performance. It encompasses:
- Business alignment and strategy
- Data governance and quality
- Technical architecture and infrastructure
- Operations and monitoring
- Security and compliance
- Team skills and organizational readiness

## 3. Continuous Improvement
Production-ready doesn't mean static. The framework supports:
- Iterative assessment cycles
- Continuous monitoring and improvement
- Feedback loops from operations
- Adaptation to changing business needs

## 4. Practical and Actionable
The framework provides:
- Clear, measurable assessment criteria
- Objective scoring methodology
- Specific recommendations for improvement
- Realistic maturity progression paths

## 5. Enterprise Focus
Tailored for enterprise environments:
- Considers organizational complexity and governance
- Addresses multi-team collaboration
- Supports regulatory compliance requirements
- Scales from startups to large enterprises

## 6. Cross-Functional Ownership
AI readiness requires accountability across functions:
- Business and strategy stakeholders
- Data and ML engineering teams
- Platform and infrastructure teams
- Operations and SRE teams
- Security and compliance teams
