# AI Readiness Maturity Model

## Overview
The EARF Maturity Model defines 5 levels of AI readiness across organizational and technical dimensions. Each level builds upon the previous one.

## Maturity Levels

### Level 1: Initial / Ad-Hoc
**Characteristics:**
- No formal AI strategy or governance
- ML/AI efforts are experimental or proof-of-concept focused
- Limited organizational awareness of AI capabilities
- Technical infrastructure is minimal or non-existent
- No formal monitoring or operational practices
- High dependency on individual contributors

**Key Indicators:**
- Ad-hoc data collection and preparation
- Manual processes for model training and deployment
- Lack of version control for models and data
- No formal testing or validation processes
- Minimal documentation

**Typical Timeline:** Early experiments (weeks to months)

---

### Level 2: Managed / Repeatable
**Characteristics:**
- Basic AI strategy defined for specific use cases
- Documented processes for common ML workflows
- Initial data governance practices in place
- Manual deployment processes with some automation
- Basic monitoring of model performance
- Emerging role specialization (data scientists, engineers)

**Key Indicators:**
- Reproducible model training pipelines
- Basic data quality checks
- Documented model versions
- Manual or script-based deployments
- Performance metrics tracked in spreadsheets or dashboards
- Post-deployment monitoring of model accuracy

**Typical Timeline:** Initial production (months to year)

---

### Level 3: Defined / Standardized
**Characteristics:**
- Comprehensive AI strategy aligned with business goals
- Standardized ML platforms and tools across organization
- Formal data governance with quality standards
- Automated CI/CD pipelines for model deployment
- Structured monitoring and alerting
- Cross-functional teams with clear responsibilities

**Key Indicators:**
- ML platform with standardized workflows
- Data catalog and lineage tracking
- Automated model validation and testing
- Infrastructure-as-code for deployments
- Comprehensive metrics and dashboards
- Formal model review and approval processes
- A/B testing frameworks

**Typical Timeline:** Scaled production (1-2 years)

---

### Level 4: Quantitatively Managed
**Characteristics:**
- AI drives significant business value and is strategically aligned
- Highly automated, self-service ML platform
- Sophisticated data governance with quality metrics
- Advanced monitoring with predictive alerting
- Established SLAs for AI systems
- Data-driven decision making for model management

**Key Indicators:**
- Quantitative model performance SLOs and SLIs
- Automated data quality monitoring and remediation
- Predictive alerting for model drift and degradation
- Automated retraining and deployment pipelines
- ROI tracking for AI initiatives
- Comprehensive security and compliance controls
- Advanced testing (adversarial, robustness, bias)

**Typical Timeline:** Operational excellence (2-3 years)

---

### Level 5: Optimized / Continuous Improvement
**Characteristics:**
- AI is integral to core business operations
- Continuous optimization of models and systems
- Proactive identification of new AI opportunities
- Advanced techniques (federated learning, transfer learning, AutoML)
- Organizational culture of AI innovation
- Strategic partnerships and ecosystem integration

**Key Indicators:**
- Automated MLOps with minimal manual intervention
- Continuous A/B testing and experimentation
- Advanced analytics and insights generation
- Automated fairness and bias monitoring
- Integration with business analytics and decision-making
- Innovation lab for emerging AI techniques
- Industry-leading practices and thought leadership

**Typical Timeline:** Industry leadership (3+ years)

---

## Progression Guidelines

### From Level 1 to 2
- Establish basic data governance
- Document your ML workflows
- Create a simple ML platform or standardized setup
- Implement basic monitoring

### From Level 2 to 3
- Invest in CI/CD infrastructure
- Centralize data management
- Implement formal model governance
- Build cross-functional teams

### From Level 3 to 4
- Implement SLOs/SLIs for AI systems
- Automate data quality and model monitoring
- Create predictive alerting systems
- Establish formal security and compliance programs

### From Level 4 to 5
- Drive organizational culture change
- Invest in advanced ML research
- Build ecosystem partnerships
- Focus on continuous optimization
